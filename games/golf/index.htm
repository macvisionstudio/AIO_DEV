THE CONCEPT

GOLF, BUILT FOR MOBILE.

This project is a mobile golf card game designed around multiplayer play. The goal is to make the game easy to learn while leaving room for strategy, competition and different ways to play.

Multiplayer is a core part of the idea, with players able to play with friends and compete against other players.

AI opponents can also provide players with a way to practice or play when other players are unavailable.

The exact rules, modes and progression systems can evolve as development continues.
