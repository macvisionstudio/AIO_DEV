Where It Came From

This isn't just a volleyball game.

Backyard Volleyball was inspired by something that actually happened in my family.

We had a large backyard, stadium lights, a volleyball net, and a family that liked to compete.

What started as weekend volleyball could turn into a full-blown nighttime event. Around thirty people might show up. Some played. Some watched. Everybody was part of it.

Games could start around 9 PM and continue until 4 or 5 in the morning.

Forget Professional Volleyball

Welcome to the chaos.

This isn't about perfectly maintained courts and professional athletes. It's about backyard competition, ridiculous saves, family rivalries and whatever happens after somebody says, "One more game."

Gameplay

Easy to learn.
Hard to survive.

Backyard Volleyball is being designed around simple controls, timing, positioning and physics rather than complicated button combinations.

Bump

Receive the ball and keep the rally alive. Timing and positioning determine the quality of the contact.

Control High

Set

Put the ball where your teammate needs it. A good set creates the opportunity for the attack.

Precision High

Spike

Time the attack and drive the ball toward the other side of the net.

Power Extreme

Dive

Throw yourself into the dirt to make the save. The most spectacular play may also be the most dangerous.

Risk High
Backyard Rules

Somebody's probably getting hurt.

The real games weren't exactly gentle. Backyard Volleyball turns that memory into an exaggerated gameplay system where collisions, bad landings and reckless dives can result in injuries and substitutions.
