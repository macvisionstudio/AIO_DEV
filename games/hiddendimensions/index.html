THE GAME

A DIFFERENT KIND OF RACING GAME

Ruzty Nutz Racing takes place on a battered world shaped by generations of racing wars.

Vehicles are rare. Wrecks are valuable. Parts can be salvaged, repaired, sold, traded, or used to build something better.

Instead of collecting dozens of disposable cars, players have to make decisions about the machines they own and invest in them.

VEHICLES

EVERY CAR MATTERS

Ruzty Nutz Racing isn't designed around owning fifty different cars.

Cars are rare enough that finding one can actually mean something.

The goal is to find a machine worth keeping, learn what it needs, and spend your resources making it yours.
