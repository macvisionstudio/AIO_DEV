The Idea

Every Day Is A New Adventure

Paisley and Koda's Big Adventures is designed around simple exploration, imagination and discovering something new around every corner.

👧 Made For Little Explorers

The game is designed around simple point-and-click interactions that are easy for young players to understand. There are no complicated controls and no need to rush.

🐶 Always With Koda

Koda isn't just a companion. He helps Paisley discover hidden objects, reacts to the world and occasionally leads the way toward the next adventure.
