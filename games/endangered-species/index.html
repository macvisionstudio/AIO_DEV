PC IN DEVELOPMENT

THE GAME

A UNIVERSE WORTH SAVING

Endangered Species is an exploration and preservation game set across distant worlds.

Land on a planet and begin searching for whatever the world has to offer: water, minerals, rocks, crystals, resources, and life.

Some discoveries can be collected, studied, preserved, or used to help build a larger understanding of the universe.

EXPLORATION

WHAT IS WAITING OUT THERE?

Every planet is an opportunity to discover something new.

01

EXPLORE

Travel across unexplored worlds and investigate unfamiliar environments.

02

DRILL

Search beneath the surface for water, minerals, crystals, rocks, and other resources.

03

DISCOVER

Find unusual forms of life and evidence of things that may have existed long before you arrived.

04

PRESERVE

Capture, preserve, and maintain copies of discoveries that may otherwise disappear forever.

LIFE

NOT EVERYTHING YOU FIND IS A RESOURCE

Different species of humans, aliens, creatures, and other forms of life can be encountered throughout the universe.

Some may be endangered. Some may be willing to join your growing alliance. Others may require protection, relocation, or preservation.

The decisions you make about what you discover will help shape the worlds you visit.

RESOURCES

EVERYTHING HAS VALUE

What you discover may become the foundation for what comes next.

01

WATER

Search for one of the most valuable resources on an unexplored world.

02

MINERALS

Extract materials that can be used for development, construction, and research.

03

CRYSTALS

Discover rare formations whose true value may not be immediately understood.

04

LIFE

Not everything valuable can be mined. Some discoveries need to be protected.

DEVELOPMENT
