THE GAME

ENDANGERED SPECIES ON THE GO

ESmobile is the lightweight mobile version of Endangered Species.

The goal is to bring the exploration, discovery, and preservation concepts of the PC game to mobile devices.

The mobile version is being designed specifically around shorter play sessions and touch controls.

DESIGN

A LIGHTER EXPERIENCE

ESmobile isn't intended to simply shrink the PC game onto a phone.

The mobile version will focus on the systems that work best for shorter sessions and touch-based interaction.

More complex systems can remain part of the larger PC experience.

THE UNIVERSE

PART OF A LARGER WORLD

ESmobile exists within the larger Endangered Species universe.

The PC and mobile versions may eventually share discoveries, characters, locations, or other elements of the same universe.
